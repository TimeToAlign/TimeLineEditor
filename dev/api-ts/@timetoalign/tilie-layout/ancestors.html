ancestorBands()#

ancestorBands(scope, ancestors, p0, edges, aligned): AncestorBands

Defined in: layout/src/ancestors.ts:278

The ancestor bands (section 8.3), one on each side holding an ancestor line, each the outermost band of its side. On a side whose outer edge is E (Ea above, Eb below), the band starts at c0 = max(p0 / 2, max(E, wm / 2) + S), S the card spacing and wm the stroke width of the member’s line, or 0 when that line is not displayed (section 6.5), and holds one row per drawn ancestor line of that side, the nearest first, each with the pitch q of its name’s font size, an inner half q / 2 and an outer half the larger of q / 2 and the reach of the wedges and labels of its named timestamps, on the side of its line away from the member. Row 1 lies at `c0

  • i1, row r + 1the distanceΔr = or + i(r + 1)beyond rowr; on the side of the root's ancestor line where its ancestor card is drawn, Δr = max(dr, or + i(r + 1)), drthe distance of the two rows' ancestors' lines in the root's drawing alone,alignedholding their ordinates there by timeline ID. The band isi1 + Δ1 + … + Δ(C − 1) + oCwide. A side without a drawn ancestor line has no band, and its edge staysE`.
