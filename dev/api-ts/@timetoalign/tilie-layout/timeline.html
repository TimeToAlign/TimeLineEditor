layoutTimeline(prepared, children, maps, aligned?): TimelineDrawing

Defined in: layout/src/timeline.ts:530

prepareRow()#

prepareRow(lines): PreparedTimeline[]

Defined in: layout/src/timeline.ts:357

The lines of a row of one band order prepared together (section 6.4, Rows of one band order), lines in traversal order, each prepared alone (prepareTimeline): the stacks of all their markers bands placed by one first fit into one set of tiers, line after line; the regions of all their regions bands by one tiered first fit, line after line, so that their rows, label tiers and pitches are one, at the largest font size of their drawn labels; the segment labels of their segment lines by one first fit into one set of segment-label tiers, at one m, which counts k when any of them draws a boundary. On each side the band of a facet is present for every line when it is present for one, and the markers and regions bands of each facet are as wide as the widest of them; the children and conversion maps bands take their spans where the row’s bands are laid out (rows.ts).

prepareTimeline()#

prepareTimeline(scope, line): PreparedTimeline

Defined in: layout/src/timeline.ts:267

The first pass for the timeline drawn with the line line (section 6.4): its band order (core’s bandOrderOf over the scope’s manifest), the side of each band, each band prepared by its module from sourcesOf (markers, conversion maps, regions, flow control; the children band from children), its markers stacks, regions and segment labels placed by its own first fits alone, the present bands’ levels, its maps, its level 0, its own extent and its centred labels. Uses no distance and no other line’s layout, so every line of a member drawing can be prepared first; prepareRow places the first fits of a row of one band order together.

sourcesOf()#

sourcesOf(scope, line): readonly Entry[]

Defined in: layout/src/timeline.ts:205

The timelines whose content the bands of the line line draw (section 6.4, Segments without a line): line itself, then, when it is a segment line, each of its displayed segments that is no segment line, in traversal order; on a switched card’s root (section 8.10), the root and its drawing in each stretch piece of its FlowMap, in their order. Such a segment’s or piece’s bands lie at line’s line: what each of its displayed bands holds joins the band of line of the same facet, after line’s own content; its own band order places nothing. None when line is not displayed, which hides its timeline’s whole drawing (section 2.4): only the root is laid out so, every other line being placed only when displayed.
