Functions#

placeItems()#

placeItems(context, block, top, aligned?, previous?, arranged?, reaches?): Item[]

Defined in: layout/src/block/places.ts:325

Every item of the block in drawing order, the frames then the block-held cards (section 8.6), block the holder of the frames and the block-held cards and top the default top of the first item, below the title’s slot and the block’s margin. An item’s length L, scale, spacing and margin are what its place states, else the placement’s length, 1, 8 for a frame and 4 for a block-held card, and 0; its member drawings are laid out at L, a block-held card’s at its item’s spacing and margin, a member’s at its own card’s (else 4 and 0), and a frame’s cards lie its spacing apart, its margin above the first and below the last. No spacing or margin multiplies another. Its height, in item coordinates, is the larger of its content’s and the requested one; its place x and y what is stated, else P and, for y, the first item at top, each further one the placement’s spacing below the largest drawn bottom edge, after the scale, of the items before it.

A frame’s member drawings are traversed first and laid out on its elided axis where its group elisions draw gaps (section 8.9), a block-held card’s on its own where its mode draws its stored card elisions. Each member card of aligned places its ancestor rows on its root’s side at the distances its ordinates give (section 8.3); an item none of whose cards it names and none of whose cards arranged pads is taken from previous where that holds it, its content kept. An arranged block’s items are taken in the order arranged gives instead of drawing order, each at its default place.

An item behind whose frame ancestor cards are drawn reaches as far as reaches gives under its place key text (section 8.8, No room): its default y lies so far lower that its cards’ top lies the placement’s spacing below the largest bottom edge of the items before it (the first item keeps its default y), and its bottom edge for the items after it is its cards’ where they reach lower than its frame, so no card overlaps another item’s.
