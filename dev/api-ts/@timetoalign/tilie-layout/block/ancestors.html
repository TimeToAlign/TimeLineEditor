alignedRows()#

alignedRows(context, block, items, columns, rowsOf): AlignedRows

Defined in: layout/src/block/ancestors.ts:352

The rows the member cards of the block align on the ancestor cards behind them (section 8.3): for every displayed ancestor card whose root’s line is drawn and whose target line is drawn in its member’s card, at the block’s columns columns, the ordinates of its root’s timelines in rowsOf of it, its root laid out alone on the gaps the card draws, carried, stored and derived alike, so that every label stack the card draws counts in dr; for each member card of that frame below that root. A card rowsOf gives nothing for aligns nothing (behind a row, only a root’s once card does, section 8.8).

ancestorCards()#

ancestorCards(context, block, items, drawings, horizontal, measure, rootOf): LaidAncestorCard[]

Defined in: layout/src/block/ancestors.ts:401

Every displayed ancestor card of the block (section 8.8), frame by frame and by its members’ display order, block the holder of the block’s own elements, items the block’s items and drawings their member drawings at the block’s start s0, rootOf each card’s root laid out alone on its elided axis (elidedRoots). R is laid out as the one ungrouped root of a block holding nothing else, at the length LA, spacing 4 and margin 0, with the name column K and its own start s0', on its elided axis (section 8.9): the gaps carried from its frame, and as its mode chooses its stored card elisions or the elisions it derives at its proportion r, which reads the frame as drawn, L' = L − DG; its card ends at Ri + K. It is moved so that its line starts at xs + DL, DL the shrinks of the gaps left of M’s span, and lies at the ordinate of R’s ancestor line in M’s card, on whichever side of M’s line R’s band order puts it (section 8.3), or of M’s line where that ancestor line is not displayed. An ancestor card whose style does not display it, or whose line or target ordinate is not drawn, is not drawn. Its lines have axes as any card’s (axesOf over R’s drawing at s0' and LA), moved with it.

behind()#

behind(context, block, items, s0): Behind[]

Defined in: layout/src/block/ancestors.ts:136

The ancestor cards of the block in the order of section 8.8, the frames taken in the order of items (the item order of an arranged block, section 8.5), whether displayed or not, at the start s0 of the block’s member drawings: for the ancestor card of the root R aligned to the member M in the frame G, ts the start of R on M’s axis (the offsets from R down to M, summed and negated), λ M’s length or reach, te = ts + λR (R’s length or reach), the proportions us = ts / λ and ue = te / λ, xs = s0 + L × us, xe = s0 + L × ue with L the frame’s length, and LA = xe − xs.

elidedRoots()#

elidedRoots(context, block, frames, s0): (at) => ElidedRoot | undefined

Defined in: layout/src/block/ancestors.ts:238

The roots of the block’s ancestor cards laid out alone on their elided axes, each once, by the card’s place key: frames the frames of the block’s items as first laid out, before any member card’s ancestor rows align on the card behind it (section 8.3), by item index, and s0 the start of their member drawings. The card’s proportion reads its frame so laid out, so that the rows the card draws, which the member cards’ rows then take (alignedRows), depend on nothing those rows move. Undefined for a card whose frame is not laid out.
