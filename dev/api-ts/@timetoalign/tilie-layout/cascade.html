Cascade#

Defined in: layout/src/cascade.ts:242

The rules of every layer, lowest rank first (section 2.5: layer, addresses, within depth, class, facet or coordinates, element, inside, the count of domain and modality, position). ancestry maps every timeline id to the ids from its root down to it, so its length is the timeline’s depth (the roots 1).
