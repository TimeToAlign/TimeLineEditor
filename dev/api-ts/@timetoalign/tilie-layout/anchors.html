anchorElements()#

anchorElements(manifest, axes, cascade, block, measure): (TextElement | AnchorElement)[]

Defined in: layout/src/anchors.ts:87

The elements of the alignment anchors of manifest’s named MatchStamps (section 8.7), the drawn lines axes in block coordinates and block the block’s style: first every anchor’s line, MatchStamp by MatchStamp in the order of matchstamps and each one’s anchors in the order of its alignment_anchors, then their labels in the same order. A line is drawn when its anchor’s display lists line, it is displayed and it has a length; the MatchStamp’s name, a label addressed {id: A, part: ["name"]} and held by the line, when display lists name and it is displayed, centred on the line’s midpoint with its baseline at ym + (a(s) − d(s)) / 2. An anchor one of whose timestamps has no cross-section segment draws nothing. Neither takes room.

anchorLine()#

anchorLine(first, second): AnchorLine | null

Defined in: layout/src/anchors.ts:53

The line of an alignment anchor whose first timestamp has the cross-section segments first and its second second (section 8.7), from a segment of the first to one of the second: of the shortest line between each pair of one segment of each, the one whose dx × dx + dy × dy is smallest, of equal ones the first in the order of first, then of second; null where either has none. A line of no length is returned too: its midpoint still places the anchor’s label. The one geometry of an alignment anchor, for a named MatchStamp’s and for any other drawn between two positions of two drawn lines (crossSectionSegment).
