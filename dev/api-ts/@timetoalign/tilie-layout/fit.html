tieredFit()#

tieredFit(items): Tiered

Defined in: layout/src/fit.ts:163

A tiered first fit (section 6.4, Regions): the items, in order, placed by their own extents alone into rows, so that a label never moves its item; and each item’s label, in the same order, by its label extent into the label tiers of its item’s row, each row filling its own tiers. A label that conflicts with one before it in its row’s first tier lies in a later tier, joined to its item by a leader line.
