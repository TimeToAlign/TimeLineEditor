LineElements#

Defined in: layout/src/bands.ts:120

What one timeline drawn with a line of its own draws (its line, and what its bands hold), by the categories of section 7.2, each category in its order within that timeline. A member drawing concatenates each category over its timelines in traversal order, so breaks, section breaks and barlines, maps, regions and jumps come band by band, and orders boundaries and segment labels by the traversal order of their segments. Ancestor lines, their marks and the row names belong to the member drawing, not to a timeline.

PreparedBand#

Defined in: layout/src/bands.ts:199

One band of a timeline once its first fits are done (section 6.4, the first of the three passes), before any distance is known. Every kind of band but the children band is prepared by its own module; the children band’s width comes from its children’s layout, the conversion maps band’s from the slots it shares with the bands of its row (JointSpans).

bandStart()#

bandStart(facet, previous, reach, w, S): number

Defined in: layout/src/bands.ts:333

The start c of a band drawing the facet facet (section 6.4, Where a band lies), previous the outer edge of the present band at the level before it on its side, undefined at level 1 or −1, reach the line’s reach on that side (Level 0), w the line’s stroke width and S the card spacing: at level 1 or −1 the markers band and the conversion maps band start at 0, every other band S beyond the larger of reach and w / 2; any further band starts at previous plus S. A children band’s own start is the larger of this and half its row’s pitch (ownStart).

fillSide()#

fillSide(side, present, reach, S, w, spans): LaidSide

Defined in: layout/src/bands.ts:363

Lay out the present bands of one side outwards from the line (section 6.4, Where a band lies), present in level order, reach the line’s reach on that side (Level 0), w the line’s stroke width and S the card spacing:

  • every band but the children band and the conversion maps band starts where bandStart puts it;

  • the children band and the conversion maps band, each laid out together with the bands of other lines of its row, start and are as wide as spans says (spans holds a span for each of them that present holds), and so does every band of a row of one band order;

  • every outer edge is the band’s start plus its width, the width summed first, and for the markers or conversion maps band at level 1 or −1 the larger of that and reach, since it shares its room with the line’s; the side’s outer edge is its outermost band’s, or reach when present is empty.

Levels are 1, 2, … above and −1, −2, … below, in the order of present.
