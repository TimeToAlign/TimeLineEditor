abscissaOf()#

abscissaOf(axis, position): number

Defined in: layout/src/axes.ts:107

The abscissa of the coordinate position on the line axis’s own axis, in block-local canvas units: xStart + (position − start) / drawnLength × (xEnd − xStart), the share computed on the exact difference and drawn length, each read as a double; xStart where the drawn length is 0. A line of unknown length places a coordinate up to its reach alike, and beyond it on the same scale.

crossedLines()#

crossedLines(drawing, lineKey): readonly string[]

Defined in: layout/src/axes.ts:243

The lines a cross-section line of the line keyed lineKey in drawing crosses, as keys of lineAxes: that line’s first, then those of its timeline’s displayed descendants drawn with a line of their own in the same member drawing, in traversal order (section 6.1), every one lying in its children band, each with every full run it is drawn in between gaps, from the left. A segment without a line of its own is no line: it lies on its segment line’s axis (LineAxis.segments). None where the key names no line, or an ancestor line.

wedgeAt()#

wedgeAt(drawing, lineKey, x): WedgeMark | null

Defined in: layout/src/axes.ts:293

The wedge at the abscissa x of the line keyed lineKey in drawing (a key of lineAxes), as a named timestamp of its timeline lying there draws its own (section 6.6, Wedges): in the line’s markers band, from where that band starts, or would start were it present; with the style of a wedge of that timeline that no named timestamp holds, turned by that style’s rotation (a rule on the band’s wedges, never one naming addresses). An ancestor line (section 8.3), which draws no named timestamp, has the mark its marks are drawn as (DrawnAxis.mark): always above the line, its tip on the line’s edge, with the style of a wedge held by the ancestor line that no named timestamp holds and that style’s rotation, so that it is faded as the marks are and no rule on the marks of the member’s start and end changes it. Null where the key names no line.
