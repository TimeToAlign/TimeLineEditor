layoutRow()#

layoutRow(row, fitted, slots): ReadonlyMap<Entry, TimelineDrawing>

Defined in: layout/src/rows.ts:316

The second pass for the lines of row (section 6.4, Children: “the distances, from the deepest rows up”), slots the slots of every present conversion maps band (rowSlots): for each side, the rows of the joint layout of its lines’ children bands, each laid out first; their halves (childrenHalves); the common start of each set of bands laid out together, the children bands and the conversion maps bands sharing slots (resolveStarts); the children placed (placeChildren); then each line of row laid out by layoutTimeline. Keyed by the row’s lines, each drawing holding its subtree.

placeLines()#

placeLines(root, row, place): PlacedLine[]

Defined in: layout/src/rows.ts:466

The third pass (section 6.4: “the ordinates, from the root’s line down”): every line of the member drawing placed, in traversal order, from the root’s drawing root in the root’s row row at place. A child in a band above its parent’s line lies at yl − lk, one below at yl + lk, yl its parent’s ordinate and lk its PlacedChild.distance; it shares x0, the top, Hc and the card’s bottom of place and takes the name size of its own row.

rowsOf()#

rowsOf(scope, lines): FirstPass

Defined in: layout/src/rows.ts:112

The first pass’s rows (section 6.4, Children): the root’s row of the member drawing and, top-down, every joint layout below it, with every row’s name size and pitch, from the lines lines prepared alone. The children bands of a joint are aligned when every child they place has one band order; each band places its children by first fit (fitChildren), by their line-only extents where aligned and their whole extents otherwise. The lines of a row placed by aligned bands are prepared together (prepareRow); every other line keeps its own preparation.
