markersBand()#

markersBand(scope, line, sources): StagedMarkers

Defined in: layout/src/markers.ts:284

The markers band of the timeline drawn with the line line (section 6.4, Markers): the named timestamps of each of sources, the line’s own first and then each joined segment’s, segment after segment, each one’s in canonical order, each at its timeline’s start plus its coordinate (section 3.1); then the secondary coordinates on each of sources of every named timestamp the card draws whose position its span holds (core’s cardStamps), source after source, in canonical order of their timestamps, each read on that source’s axis. Of each, the wedge and the labels its display lists are drawn when they are displayed and its timeline’s markers band is displayed; its labels, name, ID and coordinate (sections 5.2 and 5.3), form its stack. The stacks are placed by one first fit, in that order, into tiers counted in lines (tiersOf), each stack one item as wide as its widest label, centred on its timestamp’s X, at its unturned place; a wedge’s rotation, 0 where no rule sets it, carries its stack (carried). After them come the label stacks of the gaps lying on its sources, each centred on L × um at full scale and as many lines high as the last line it is drawn on below its ellipsis (stackLines), so a stack moved to a later level takes that room. The band holds its wedges and its labels directly and is present when it draws either; it is w / 2 + 13 wide without a label, w / 2 + 13 + 2 + T × h(9) with T ≥ 1 lines of labels, w the stroke width of the line and 9 the largest font size of its drawn labels; it adds to its timeline’s extent [L × u + a, L × u + b] for every wedge it draws, a and b the smallest and largest of 0 and its corners’ offsets across ([−3, 3] unturned), and the extent of every stack where its wedge carries it. A cross-section line is held by its timeline’s line, not by the band: it is drawn when its timestamp’s display lists it and its own display is inline, whatever the band’s, and takes no room. A wedge, its labels and its cross-section line at a position in a gap are not drawn (section 6.6, Elisions), and that position takes its room all the same.

stampLabels(timestamp, timeline, own, styledLabel, measured): StampLabel[]

Defined in: layout/src/markers.ts:146

wedgeElement()#

wedgeElement(address, style, x, at, side, start, w): MarkerElement

Defined in: layout/src/markers.ts:93

A wedge at x of the markers band of the line placed at at, the band starting at start outwards on side and w the line’s stroke width (section 6.6, Wedges): its tip start + w / 2 out, and each corner of its base, turned by the style’s rotation (0 where unset) about the tip, at its offset across and its distance beyond the tip’s, that distance added to the tip’s before it becomes an ordinate; unturned, its base 13 further out and 6 wide.
