levelZero()#

levelZero(scope, line): StagedZero

Defined in: layout/src/line.ts:104

Level 0 of the timeline drawn with the line line (sections 6.1, 6.4 and 6.6): its segments are its children when it is a segment line, every one of them, with a line of its own or not; their labels are placed by one first fit, in canonical order, into the segment-label tiers above the line. A segment’s boundary and label are held by its segment line, directly and in no band (section 2.1), and drawn only with that line.
