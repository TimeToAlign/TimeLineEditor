drawMaps()#

drawMaps(members, slots, start, side, at): Pick<LineElements, "maps">

Defined in: layout/src/maps.ts:138

The maps of a conversion maps band starting at start outwards on side of the line placed at at, in the order they are taken (section 6.4, Conversion maps): the map in slot j centred c + w / 2 + j × (g + d) − d / 2 out from the line, summed in that order, w the slots’ largest stroke width, spanning its own timeline from Xs to Xe. A map whose start lies in a gap starts at the gap’s right edge, one whose end does ends at its left edge, and one whose start and end so meet or cross is not drawn (section 6.6, Elisions).

mapMembers(scope, sources): MapMember[]

Defined in: layout/src/maps.ts:56
