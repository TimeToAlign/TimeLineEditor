frameElisions()#

frameElisions(members, group, holder, L): FrameElisions | undefined

Defined in: layout/src/elisions.ts:1056

The group elisions of the frame holder of the group group (section 8.9): the stored elisions whose timelines lie in the group, in canonical order, each where its gap is displayed and its timeline’s line is drawn in the frame, while the frame’s elision-mode is auto or custom. Each elision’s stretch, carried to the axis of the member M it lies under, becomes the exact proportions [P, Q] of M’s length or reach (the exact quotients, a float at its exact binary value), clipped to [0, 1]; each draws what of its stretch lies outside every piece drawn before it, compared exactly, so an end where an earlier piece cut it is that piece’s exact end (Gap.exact) and the gaps carried into an ancestor card meet exactly where the frame’s meet. The doubles p and q nearest them place the gap, at the natural width n = L × q − L × p and the drawn width its width sets (span read with M’s length); an elision that is not drawn takes nothing from the elisions after it. A piece’s elided coordinates on its timeline are the elision’s own start and end where it is the whole stretch; a cut piece’s, on an exact axis, the exact positions at P and Q less the timeline’s start, written in its number type, and on a float axis p × λ less that start in binary64. Undefined where it draws no gap.

stackLines()#

stackLines(axis): ReadonlyMap<Gap, number>

Defined in: layout/src/elisions.ts:1334

The line, from 1, on which the label stack of each gap of axis drawing one starts below its ellipsis (section 6.6): one first fit (section 6.3) of the stacks, in the order of the gaps from the left, into tiers counted in lines, each stack one item as many lines high as it has labels, its extent that of one label as wide as its widest label centred on the gap’s middle xm, L × u − D standing for xm − x0. A tier is as many lines high as its highest stack, and a stack starts on its tier’s first line: so a stack that would cover a neighbouring gap’s drawn below the same ellipses moves to the next level, and no gap widens for it. A gap draws its stack where it is displayed and lists a label, a frame’s gap across its frame and a card’s gap where its timeline’s markers band takes the stack’s room (Gap.entry). Memoised by the axis.
