inside#

readonly inside: readonly ElementType[]

Defined in: core/src/manifest/elements.ts:70

Its holder chain: the types of the elements holding it in the tree (sections 2.1 and 8.1), outermost first, which the selector member inside tests. A key’s chain is its holder’s chain followed by the holder’s type, so build keys with elementKey, textKey, bandKey and unownedKey from the holder’s key; a segment that is not a segment line (type null) adds nothing. Two drawings of one address differ here only.

name#

const name: "@timetoalign/tilie-layout"

Defined in: layout/src/index.ts:12

@timetoalign/tilie-layout: the drawing of one TimeToShare manifest.

draw(manifest, placement, layers, metrics) implements TimeToShare’s drawing specification (docs/drawing.md, sections 1 to 6 and 8): the cascade of style layers, exact positions, text measured from the font’s advance widths, the layout of bands and rows, and the block of section 8 that holds every manifest, each root in its card. Pure and deterministic.
