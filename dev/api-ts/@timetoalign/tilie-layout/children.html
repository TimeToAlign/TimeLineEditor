fitChildren(lines, extentOf): ChildrenFit

Defined in: layout/src/children.ts:57

The first fit of a children band (section 6.4, Children): the child lines lines, in the order PreparedTimeline.children gives them, each placed as a whole by its extent extentOf into the lowest row holding no child whose extent conflicts with it (section 6.3).
