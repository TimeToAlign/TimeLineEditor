ElisionElement#

Defined in: layout/src/elements.ts:319

The gap of an elision (sections 6.6 and 8.9): the rectangle from x(a) to x(b) and from the top yt of its card to its bottom yb (across a frame, from the top of its topmost member card to the bottom of its bottommost), filled and not stroked, drawn only where its fill is not none; and its continuations, one on each row that crosses it, at the rows’ ordinates, where its display lists continuation. It carries what it leaves out on the timeline it lies on and where it comes from: a derived elision, which no manifest stores, is read from here.
