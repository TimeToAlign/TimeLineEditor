maps/timestamp#

Interfaces#

CrossSectionMember#

Defined in: maps/timestamp.ts:112

A timeline a timestamp may hold: its zero lies offset from the anchor’s zero, on the anchor’s axis, exactly. A member of a group the anchor is a member of, read by the group (a groupstamp’s), has a scale too: the members of a group start and end together, so its coordinate is the anchor’s times its drawn length over the anchor’s. Its coordinate at a position p on the anchor’s axis is (p − offset) × scale, scale 1 where it is not given. Where the length of such a member, or of the anchor’s member, is not known, the group gives it no coordinate (one member’s length is never carried to another): it is valueless, its scale (a drawn length over another) only says where its drawing is crossed, and none of its rows has a value.

CrossSectionSpec#

Defined in: maps/timestamp.ts:165

What a timestamp is read from: the anchor, the members its cross-section may hold (the anchor excluded, in the order given: by the canvas’s convention the anchor’s ancestors, parent first, then the timelines its line crosses), and the granularity its floats are shown with and its pointer positions picked to. snapOnRow()#

snapOnRow(spec, row, p, h, knots?): Rational

Defined in: maps/timestamp.ts:520

The position on the anchor’s axis a pointer picks on the row row (exact): p is the pointer, in units from the anchor’s zero, h half a device pixel in the anchor’s units.

  • A member of an int axis (samples, ticks, pixels), on its coordinate’s row and on every map’s row alike: the integer nearest to p minus the offset (a tie going up), the offset added, so such a timeline steps by 1 whatever the granularity; no map’s value axis decides its step.

  • Any other member’s own coordinate: the simplest value of the member’s number type within h of p minus the offset (snapValue), the offset added.

  • A map’s value of any other member: v, the map’s value at p, is snapped within hv, half the absolute difference of its values at p − h and p + h, under the cap of the map’s target number type (the value’s own kind where the map names no target unit); the snapped value is inverted exactly (RowAxis.positionOf). A map without an inverse there, a value that is no number, or one whose inverse gives nothing falls back to the member’s own coordinate.

The result is held to the member’s span within the anchor’s [0, length].

timestampAt()#

timestampAt(spec, position, knots?): Timestamp

Defined in: maps/timestamp.ts:444

The timestamp of position, exact on the anchor’s axis: the anchor’s part, then the part of each member, in the order given, whose closed span [offset, offset + length] holds the position (a boundary two contiguous members share is crossed by both). Each part’s coordinate is the position minus its offset, written in its number type, and each map is evaluated there in the order given. knots are the expanded rows of the manifest’s MeasureMaps, or why they are not at hand (no_server when not given); a map reading a MeasureMap without its rows gives no value and says why (failed when the rows given lack it).

timestampRows()#

timestampRows(pin): TimestampRow[]

Defined in: maps/timestamp.ts:679

The rows of pin’s timestamp in order: its parts top to bottom where the reading crosses each one’s timeline (its ordinate in the spec), and within a part its rows top to bottom as they are drawn: its coordinate at that ordinate, each map at its entry of the timeline’s mapOrdinates. Both sorts are stable, every part or row without an ordinate after those with one, so a part without ordinates lists its coordinate, then its maps in drawing order. A coordinate is editable; a map while it is invertible over its timeline’s span. Every row of a valueless member has no value, says VALUELESS_TEXT instead and is not editable. The timestamp’s own parts keep the anchor first.
