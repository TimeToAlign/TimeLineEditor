addressWords()#

addressWords(view, address): string

Defined in: workspace/address.ts:720

An address as a message names it to a reader: the workspace, a bundle by its manifest’s name or id (bundle 'Mixed', the anonymous bundle), an element by its kind and its name, else its ID (timeline 'Score', region 'region_1'), a band and a card by their timeline (the regions band of timeline 'Score'), a part by its owner, innermost first (the coordinate of timestamp 'Downbeat', a bundle’s or a group’s name its title). An element of a manifest is followed by its bundle (in bundle 'Mixed') unless the view holds that block alone. An address naming nothing is worded from its members: its ID quoted ('gone'), its block by its bundle, or by its ID (block '0000000009') when the view does not hold it. addressKey, not this, is an address’s string form.

resolveElement()#

resolveElement(manifest, element): ResolvedElement | undefined

Defined in: workspace/address.ts:489

Resolve an element address against a manifest: a frame, a measure map, a timeline, a timeline’s cards, a band (of a timeline drawn in a card), an entry, or a part that the manifest’s drawing draws (ManifestIndex.drawn, docs/drawing.md section 2.2): the manifest’s title (of a named manifest), a group’s title, the name of a timeline drawn with one, the wedges of a member drawing’s named timestamps with their labels, a segment’s boundary (of every segment but the first) and label, the label of a region, a break, a section break, a barline or a jump, a named timestamp’s label and cross-section line (line), a stored elision’s ellipsis and labels, and the cards. A named timestamp and a stored elision themselves resolve with the timeline they lie on, drawn or not; a named MatchStamp and an alignment anchor, which belong to no timeline, resolve by their IDs, the anchor with the MatchStamp holding it. A derived elision, which the drawing derives and which has no ID, resolves as the part elision of the timeline T it lies on ({id: T, part: ["elision"]}, its ellipsis ["elision", "ellipsis"], its labels ["elision", "name"] and ["elision", "coordinate"]) wherever T may carry one (defaultElisionTimelines), never as an invented ID. undefined for anything else, the bundle’s address {} included (the workspace resolver reads it as the block).
