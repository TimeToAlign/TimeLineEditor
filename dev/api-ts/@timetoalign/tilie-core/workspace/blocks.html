ItemArrangement#

Defined in: workspace/blocks.ts:39

How a block’s items, its groups and ungrouped roots, are arranged instead of standing at their places: packed one under another (vertical) or side by side (horizontal, every root the groups hang on drawn once behind the row), in the order order gives (itemOrder: IDs naming no item are passed over, items it leaves out follow in drawing order).
