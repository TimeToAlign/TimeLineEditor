cardStamps()#

cardStamps(manifest, member, frame?): CardStamps

Defined in: manifest/elements.ts:916

The timelines and named timestamps of the card drawing member, in the frame frame when the card is a member card there (null for an ungrouped root’s card and an ancestor card, section 8.8, which no groupstamp line crosses). Every position is taken on the axis of the member’s outermost ancestor, the member’s start being the sum of the offsets from there down, so that no value is negated. A timeline of the member’s tree whose length is not known ends where it is drawn to (section 3.4): at its reach, or, a segment, at the next segment’s start or its segment line’s end; an ancestor whose length is not known has no end. A groupstamp of another member O of the frame, one of O’s own timestamps, lies on this card at the proportion of the member’s drawn length at which it lies on O’s (section 6.6); where the member’s length or O’s is not known, its secondary coordinates there have no value. Memoised by manifest, member and frame.

manifestElements()#

manifestElements(manifest): ElementKey[]

Defined in: manifest/elements.ts:673

Every element of a manifest’s drawing, in the order of its tree (sections 2.1 and 8.1): the block; the title of a named manifest (one with an id or a name), addressed {part: ["name"]}; the ancestor cards behind the frames (section 8.8), each an ancestor_card at {id: R, part: ["card"]} followed by the member drawing of its root R, all held by the block, whether a rule displays them or not, each followed by the gaps carried into it from the frame G it stands behind (section 8.9), one per group elision of G (groupElisions) in canonical order, each with its ellipsis and no label, keyed as the frame’s own gap of it (held by the frame), whose cascaded values it takes; each group’s frame, its title and its member drawings in display order; then the roots drawn without a frame; the cards of the ancestor cards and of those roots holding the gaps of the stored card elisions of their timelines (memberElements); then, after every item, the gaps of the frames’ group elisions, frame by frame in drawing order and each frame’s in canonical order, each {id: E} of type elision held by the frame with its ellipsis and its labels (gapElements), belonging to the elision’s timeline; then the lines of the groupstamps across their groups’ frames (section 6.6), so that every drawn line has an address: timestamp by timestamp in the order of graphical.timestamps, for each groupstamp whose display lists cross_section, one cross_section at its line’s address {id: X, part: ["line"]} per frame holding a card that draws its timeline (the member itself or a descendant of it), held by that frame and belonging to the timestamp’s timeline, in the order of the frames; then the alignment anchors of the named MatchStamps (section 8.7), MatchStamp by MatchStamp and each one’s anchors in the order of its alignment_anchors: each anchor’s line {id: A}, which the block holds, and, when its display lists name, the MatchStamp’s name at it, a label addressed {id: A, part: ["name"]} that the line holds. A timeline drawn more than once, as an ancestor line and as a line of its own, or as a member of several groups, is listed at each drawing with the same address, cards included; so is an elision drawn in several cards or frames. The elisions a card derives (section 8.9) depend on the drawing’s geometry and are listed nowhere.

The list is a list of keys, each element once per drawing of it with its holder, never the order in which a drawing writes its elements (section 8.5): a card’s stored gaps stand here after the bands of their timeline, where section 8.5 writes them after the card’s whole member drawing, from the left. Read it as a set of keys wherever an order would be compared.

reachOf()#

reachOf(timeline, manifest, reaches?): Real

Defined in: manifest/elements.ts:851

The reach of a timeline on its own axis (section 3.4): the largest of its number type’s zero and every coordinate its content reaches, displayed or not. A child counts its offset plus its length, or plus its own reach when its length is not known; regions their start and end; every flow-control element its coordinate, and a jump the place each destination with a by reaches, its coordinate plus that distance (every other destination lands on a coordinate counted here, a region’s start, a child’s offset or 0); every named timestamp of it in manifest’s graphical object its coordinate. Maps are not content, and a measure map belongs to no timeline. reaches holds the reach of every timeline already walked, so one traversal walks each subtree once.

regionClasses()#

regionClasses(manifest, region): readonly string[] | undefined

Defined in: manifest/elements.ts:215

The classes the region region names by its type statement (section 2.3), each in every spelling of a selector’s class that stands for the same IRI, in the order of the statement. A stated class stands for the IRI it expands to under the manifest’s @context (expandIri), else for itself; a selector’s class holding : stands for the format’s namespace followed by rest when it is tts:rest, whatever the context declares, else for the IRI it expands to under the context, else for itself. So a region’s spellings are those of its IRI, tts:rest and p:rest for every prefix p the context declares, that stand for it. undefined for a region stating no type.
