groupsHolding()#

groupsHolding(manifest, timeline, ancestry?): readonly object[]

Defined in: manifest/tree.ts:131

The groups of manifest having the timeline timeline as a member, in the order of groups: those whose members list it or one of its ancestors, since a member’s descendants are drawn inside the member’s card in the group’s frame. An ancestor of a listed member is in none of them; a timeline the manifest does not hold is in none. ancestry is the manifest’s timelineAncestry, given where the caller holds it.
