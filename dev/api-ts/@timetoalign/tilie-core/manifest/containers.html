ContainerRef#

Defined in: manifest/containers.ts:216

One drawing of a container of a manifest. A frame is drawn once; a card once per frame holding it, or once on the block, and a root’s ancestor card once behind each frame holding a member below it; a band once in each card that draws its timeline, never in an ancestor card. Every drawing of a container carries its container’s address, so the cards of one timeline share theirs: the key tells them apart.

Functions#

containersOf()#

containersOf(manifest): Containers

Defined in: manifest/containers.ts:288

List every container of manifest, memoised by manifest identity, in drawing order: the ancestor cards behind the frames (memberDrawings), each held by the block, keyed {item: G, member: R} and holding no band; then per group in canonical order its frame, then per member in display order the member’s card followed by the bands of the timelines the card holds; then the same for every ungrouped root’s card. A timeline that several groups have as a member has a card in each of their frames, each followed by its own drawing of the same bands. A card holds the bands of its timeline and of every descendant down to, not into, a descendant drawn in a card of its own (a member of another group); each timeline’s five bands follow in FACETS order, the timelines in pre-order, children by offset then ID. A timeline drawn in no card (a root that holds a member, and the descendants of such a root that hold none) has no bands here: nothing draws them.
