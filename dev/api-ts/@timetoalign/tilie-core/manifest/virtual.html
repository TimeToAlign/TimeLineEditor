resolveVirtualTimeline()#

resolveVirtualTimeline(manifest, virtualId): ResolvedVirtualTimeline

Defined in: manifest/virtual.ts:110

The virtual timeline virtualId of manifest resolved: its unit and number type as stated, its length, and the map it rests on. A FlowMap is resolved against its root (resolveFlowMap); its length is the virtual length written in the stated number type, also where through a group carries the FlowMap to another member (its axis is the FlowMap’s root’s, as timetoalign reads it); where through names anything else (a conversion map, not evaluated), the stated length, the FlowMap’s positions staying on its root’s axis. A conversion map of a timeline’s conversion_maps is evaluated by core into the virtual timeline’s unit, its length the map’s value at the end of the timeline holding it.

Parameters#

manifest#

Manifest

virtualId#

string

Returns#

ResolvedVirtualTimeline

Throws#

unknown_virtual_timeline; as resolveFlowMap and virtual_unresolved for a FlowMap; unit_mismatch where a FlowMap applied without through or through a group lies on another unit; map_not_evaluated, naming the map, where core does not evaluate the conversion map onto the virtual timeline’s unit (a measure view, a label, named outputs, another target unit); unknown_length where its timeline’s length is unknown.
