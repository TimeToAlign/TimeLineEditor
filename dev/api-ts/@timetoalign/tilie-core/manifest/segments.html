manifest/segments#

Interfaces#

Gap#

Defined in: manifest/segments.ts:38

How the gap a removed segment leaves is closed. left extend: the segment before it grows by its length. right extend: the segment after it starts where it started and grows by its length. right shift: every later segment moves back by its length and the segment line shortens by it. left shift: every earlier segment moves on by its length, which the segment line’s offset in its parent grows by while the line shortens by it, so the later segments keep their place in the parent.

segmentGaps()#

segmentGaps(manifest, id): readonly GapOption[] | undefined

Defined in: manifest/segments.ts:229

The four ways of closing the gap removing segment id leaves, in the order of GAPS, each with why it is refused or null when it is permitted; undefined when id is no segment. Extending needs a neighbour on that side whose length may change (one not locked, or of unknown length; a segment that is no segment line itself is never locked); shifting needs the segment line not locked, and shifting left needs it to be a child. A way whose result the manifest’s rules refuse (a segment line that is itself a segment changing its length, a child leaving its parent) is refused with that rule’s message. A way is named by the neighbour that moves: “left shifts” moves the line’s offset in its parent.
