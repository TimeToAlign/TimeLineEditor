Stacking#

Defined in: arranger/src/boxes.ts:155

The order a block node shows its drawing in, from bottom to top, with the ancestor cards of the root raised raised (raisedRoot, else null): the ancestor cards that are not raised (docs/drawing.md in TimeToShare, section 8.8, in record order, a later one over an earlier one), then every other frame and card (one level: a member’s card lies in its frame, and items laid over one another by the user cover nothing here), then the raised ancestor cards, in record order. The one statement of that order: the block node draws by it (raisedLayers), the pointer and every place of a coordinate read lines by covered, the marquee and an editor’s focus leave out what lies behind.

holdingOf()#

holdingOf(drawing, selected): Holding

Defined in: arranger/src/boxes.ts:746

The boxes of drawing holding part of the selection selected (element addresses by their keys, as the descriptors carry them): a displayed frame or card holds the selection when an element of its range (DrawnBox.range) carries a selected address, the box itself excepted. A card holds its member drawing, its timeline and everything drawn with it; a frame its title, its members’ cards and what they hold; the block’s own box every drawn element. Where a box lies plays no part, so items that overlap change nothing. So a selected card marks its frame and the block but not itself; a line marks its card, that card’s frame and the block; and an address drawn in several cards, such as an ancestor line, marks each of them. An address that is not drawn marks nothing. A band, which draws nothing, counts as its timeline’s line. A group elision’s gap, written after every item, is held by its frame (and, carried, by each ancestor card behind it).
