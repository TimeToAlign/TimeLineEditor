clickedAt()#

clickedAt(selection, pressed, editing, __namedParameters): Selection

Defined in: arranger/src/selectionRules.ts:241

The selection after a click on pressed: what the press selects (pressedIn), alone, or, when additive (Shift, or the marquee toggle), toggled in the selection (click). A click on the empty canvas, and in an editor a click outside the edited element (isOutside: on its group, on anything else faded, on another block), clears the selection in every mode, the marquee mode included; with Shift it leaves it. Anything else (a control of the canvas) leaves it.

isOutside()#

isOutside(pressed, editing): boolean

Defined in: arranger/src/selectionRules.ts:195

Whether pressed lies outside the edited element: the empty pane (which a faded block, taking no pointer, lets a press through to), or a part of a block where a press selects nothing (pressedIn, the inside test that also fades), so never an offered band. Nothing lies outside on the arranger; the canvas’s own controls (toolbars, handles) lie in no block and are not the pane.
