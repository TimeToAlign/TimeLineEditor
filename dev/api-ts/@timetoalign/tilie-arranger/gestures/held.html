heldGesture()#

heldGesture(host, group, member): GestureStrategy<HeldPress, HeldValue>

Defined in: arranger/src/gestures/held.ts:187

The drag of the card of member held by the frame of group. A press is taken while the canvas reorders cards (rulesOf(...).reorder), except on a pinned card (isPinned); the press that becomes a drag selects the card. A drop the arrangement refuses (heldRefusal) previews the stored order, stores nothing and shows its reason (NodeActions.refuse). The order follows the pointer’s drawing point through the drawing as stored at the press (memberOrderAt); the other cards slide apart in the preview while a copy of the dragged card’s drawing follows the pointer, the card itself left hidden where it would drop. The release stores the order in one set (reorderCommand), nothing for a release in place. However the gesture ends, the copy then moves from where it was dropped to where its card is drawn (releaseMotion), the card hidden until it arrives; at once under reduced motion.
