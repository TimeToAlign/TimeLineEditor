itemGesture()#

itemGesture(host, key): GestureStrategy<ItemPress, Point>

Defined in: arranger/src/gestures/item.ts:48

The item drag of the item key. A press is taken while the canvas moves items (rulesOf(...).move). When the press becomes a drag, an item in the selection moves with every other item in it by the same travel and leaves the selection as it is (itemsMovedWith); an item outside it moves alone and is selected.

The item’s place moves by the pointer’s screen travel since the press, divided by the canvas zoom into canvas units and by the block’s scale into drawing units, both as they were at the press, and quantized to GRID_FREE. The drawing’s screen CTM is not read: an engine lays the <svg> out at its own layout unit, so the CTM’s scale differs from the zoom times the scale in the last digits, and a whole-pixel travel would not come out as a whole number of units.
