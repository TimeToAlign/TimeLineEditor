flowPaneOf()#

flowPaneOf(block, drawing, graphical, metrics, root, rows): FlowPane | null

Defined in: arranger/src/flowEditorRows.ts:157

The pane of the flow editor of root in block, drawn as drawing with graphical and metrics, its rows rows: null where the root’s card is not drawn. Each row is drawn by layout as the card of a virtual timeline applying its FlowMap to the root, a block-held card of its own whose graphical length is the root card’s item length times the FlowMap’s played length over the root’s drawn length (so a played and a written stretch of one length are equally long, unless runs or elisions shorten them), at the item’s scale, its line moved to start where the root’s card’s line does.
