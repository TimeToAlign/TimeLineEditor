chunkLights()#

chunkLights(manifest, drawing, chunk): ChunkLights

Defined in: arranger/src/ribbons.ts:247

The ribbons of chunk in drawing, the drawing of manifest, and the chunks it lights; nothing where its virtual timeline does not resolve through a FlowMap of its own root (resolveVirtualTimeline), or where the chunk is a gap.

For every brace of the chunk drawn (one per card drawing its virtual timeline: a run drawn once draws only its first entry’s, section 8.10) and every stretch the chunk plays (one, or a virtual timeline’s several), the ribbon leaves the card’s line of the virtual timeline over the stretch’s share of the brace, x1 + share × (x2 − x1) at the played start and end, the chunk’s span from its first stretch’s played start to its last’s played end; and reaches every line of the source root drawn in a card that is not switched, over the written stretch clipped to what that line draws (abscissaOf). Ribbons come in the order of the braces, then the stretches, then the source’s lines.

It lights the brace of every other chunk, of any virtual timeline drawn, that names the same source.
