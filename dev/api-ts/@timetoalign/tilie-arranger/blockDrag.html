BlockDrag#

Defined in: arranger/src/blockDrag.ts:156

The arranger’s block drag over Svelte Flow’s: the dragged nodes snap to the grid and to the other blocks, fixed for one gesture; the release stores one move, or shows the projection again when nothing is stored. Dragging a block that is not selected selects it, unless the selection lies inside it. On an arranged canvas nothing snaps: the leading block follows the pointer while the others are shown at their places in the order its drop gives (arrangedDrop), and the release stores that order as one new index of the leading block (blockIndexCommand).
