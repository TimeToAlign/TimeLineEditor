Stretch#

Defined in: arranger/src/items.ts:353

A stretch of one frame or card of a block on one axis, as a function of the factor’s value (length along time, spacing or margin vertically), built from one drawing of the block before the stretch and memoized per value, so a search and the changes it settles on draw each value once.

At a value, the factor is stated on its key (a held card stretches its group’s length and its own spacing or margin, resizingOf), and every stated size of the boxes stretched follows its own content by kept, keeping its proportion to it: along time the widths of the item and of the cards its frame holds, so the members’ lines keep one length and every margin stretches with them; vertically the heights of a frame and its cards, or of a held card and its frame; the cards first, since the item’s content holds them. Where the level pins the frame’s width (frameWidthPinned), a stretch along time keeps the frame at its drawn width while the content, the cards’ widths following, fits in it (its width stated at that width), and lets the frame follow the content once the content is wider (its width unstated), so a stretch shrinks the members inside the frame and grows the frame with them. Sizes are in item coordinates.

itemHandlesFor(mode, box, level): readonly Handle[]

Defined in: arranger/src/items.ts:131

The handles box, a frame or a card, offers in mode at level. A frame or a block-held card offers a block’s: the corners to scale, all eight to size or stretch its box; where the level pins the frame’s width (frameWidthPinned), its stretch leaves out the left edge and the left corners. itemResizeValues()#

itemResizeValues(view, block, box, level, mode, handle, delta, metrics, pane?): PlaceChanges

Defined in: arranger/src/items.ts:535

The places that resize box, a frame or a card of block block, by dragging handle by delta (canvas units, as for a block) at level: each key of places with its values, in order. Every position is in the drawing’s units, measured from the place origin; every size in the item’s own coordinates, before its scale, so the pointer’s travel is divided by the block’s scale and the item’s; every number is quantized to GRID_FREE. The dragged edge or corner ends under the pointer and the opposite one stays exactly where it is drawn: for a left or top handle the stated x or y is the opposite edge less the achieved drawn size.

  • scale (corners only) sets the item’s scale to its scale times the larger of the two axis ratios of the dragged corner’s distance from the fixed corner after and before the drag, floored at MIN_SCALE; scale 1, the default, unstates it. On a held card the fixed corner is its frame’s top left and the dragged corner the card’s bottom right, and the scale is the group’s.

  • box sets width and height; a request at or below the box’s own content stores null. On a held card they are the card’s own.

  • stretch sets length from a left or right handle and, from a top or bottom one, spacing, or margin for a container with exactly one content (verticalStretchOf), both from a corner: the largest value whose box does not pass the pointer, floored at MIN_LENGTH and at 0, the places being what a Stretch to it writes. Vertically a frame’s spacing is the gap between its cards and a box-held card’s the gap between its rows and bands, a margin the empty space above its one content and below it, and a value the search leaves where it was drawn is not stated; a held card stretches its group along time, the card’s own box following the pointer, and only its own card’s spacing or margin vertically. Where the frame keeps its width (Stretch), a stretch along time makes the frame’s content follow the pointer, whichever box is dragged; vertically the frame adapts.

placeEdit()#

placeEdit(view, selection, property, value, editing, metrics, pane?): SetCommand | Problem | null

Defined in: arranger/src/items.ts:731

The one set the inspector dispatches for a typed or stepped property: property of every block, frame or card the selection names (core’s resolver, resolveSelection where editing, the innermost open editor, works: a part of a group or of a timeline drawn in a card names that frame or card; bands are left out) given value, or value(current), current being the element’s shown value (stated, else drawn). A block sets its own member; a frame or a card a member of the place its resizing writes (resizingOf: a held card’s scale and length are its group’s), each place once, with the first element’s value. A length, spacing or margin writes what a Stretch to it writes (Stretch), each element’s stretch starting from what the ones before it wrote; and where the level’s items lie freely (itemsFree), the same set states the drawn position of every other item that states none (placeCommand). null when every element stores that value already.

Refused (CommandError): a selection naming no block, frame or card (unknown_target when nothing resolves, wrong_target_kind for an element with no geometry of its own, elements in several blocks, or blocks mixed with frames or cards), a position of a held card (unknown_property), and a value that is not finite or, except a width or height, empty (invalid_value).
