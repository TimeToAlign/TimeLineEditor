arrangedOf(view, editing): Arranged | null

Defined in: arranger/src/containers.ts:105

How the contents of the level under editing are arranged, as drawn: on the arranger the workspace’s arrangement of its blocks (a matrix’s columns columnsOf the number of blocks); in the bundle editor of a block its items’ arrangement, vertical where horizontal is stated but the rules refuse it (the block is then drawn one item under another, core’s rowOf); null where the contents lie at their places, and in a group or timeline editor, whose cards keep their own order.

fieldsOf()#

fieldsOf(box, level): readonly PlaceField[]

Defined in: arranger/src/containers.ts:301

The place members of box at level, in core’s order of a place, each with whether the level edits it and whose value it is (RESIZING). A member a resize changes is edited where the container resizes (resizingOf), and the block’s own size wherever its block is edited, since every editor adapts the block’s own box. A position is the block’s or an item’s: the block’s is edited at every level, an item’s in an editor whose level moves what it places (LEVELS, the bundle editor); a card a frame holds has none, its frame placing it. A band and an ancestor card have no place: none.

rulesOf()#

rulesOf(editing, mode): LevelRules

Defined in: arranger/src/containers.ts:157

What the canvas permits under editing, the innermost open editor (the arranger’s rules without one): a locked canvas changes no placement, so it moves and nudges nothing, moves no band and resizes nothing; a read-only canvas writes nothing, so it also reorders, removes, raises or lowers nothing, opens no editor (an opened editor is journalled) and offers no lock. Selecting, the marquee, panning and zooming stay. Where the level moves its contents and they are arranged (arrangedOf), it moves and nudges none freely and carries the arrangement instead, which the lock and the read-only mode turn off as they turn off moving. The one answer to “may this run”.
