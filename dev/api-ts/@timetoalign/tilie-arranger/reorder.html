orderAt()#

orderAt(entries, dragged, point, direction): string[]

Defined in: arranger/src/reorder.ts:59

The order entries (every content of a container, in their order, each where it is drawn in that order) take when dragged is dropped at point: dragged before the first other content that does not precede the point, or last. Along vertical a content precedes the point when its middle lies at or above it; along horizontal at or left of it; in a matrix, read row by row, when its row (the contents sharing its top) lies above the point’s (the last row whose top lies at or above the point, the first where none does), or it lies in the point’s row with its middle at or left of it. The contents do not move under the pointer while it searches: entries are where they lie before the drop.

rowRefusal()#

rowRefusal(manifest, order, moved): string | null

Defined in: arranger/src/reorder.ts:192

Why the items of manifest cannot stand side by side in the order order that moving the item moved gives (core’s itemOrder of it), where the item would leave its place among the stretches of the roots it hangs on (the drawing specification’s “Drawn once”: along every root the stretches follow the row from left to right): for each root of moved, in its group’s display order, "G" stays right of "A", "B" and left of "C" on "R", naming in the order’s order the groups whose stretches lie wholly before its own (it stays right of them) and wholly after (left), where the order puts them on the wrong side; clauses joined by ; . null where moved keeps every such place: an ungrouped root, or a group hanging on nothing, is never refused.
