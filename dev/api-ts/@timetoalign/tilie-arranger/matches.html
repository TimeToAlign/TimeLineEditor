matchFlag()#

matchFlag(index, manifest, lines, stamp, decimals, groups?): MatchFlagGroup[]

Defined in: arranger/src/matches.ts:734

The flag of the potential MatchStamp stamp: every coordinate of it, and with groups (above a timeline editor) every coordinate its groupstamps read on the other members of their groups (groups.groupstampAt: the scaled members of the coordinate’s GroupStamp pin, the arranger’s timelinePin read by the group, as the Matchstamp tab’s Coordinates read it), each value once per timeline. Each coordinate is a row written as core’s prettyText with decimals places, in the timeline’s unit, but a member the group gives no value (a length of the group not known: the pin’s member valueless), written core’s VALUELESS_TEXT, as the coordinate flag and the stamp tabs write it; rows in the order they are drawn, top to bottom, then left to right (a coordinate of the stamp at its screenPlace over the drawing lines were read from, with the root raised as they were read; a groupstamp’s at the ordinate its pin reads the member at, on its coordinate’s abscissa), what the block does not draw last, in the index’s order. The rows are grouped by runs: one group for each run of rows on one timeline, named as manifest names it (its ID where it holds none), so the flag reads top to bottom exactly as drawn, a timeline whose rows lie apart in that order heading each run, and one whose rows are adjacent heading them once.

stampOverlay()#

stampOverlay(index, lines, stamp, groupstampAt?): MatchOverlay

Defined in: arranger/src/matches.ts:513

Every line and connector of the potential MatchStamp stamp in a block, as a named one would draw them: each of its coordinates on every line holding it, and where groupstampAt says naming would store it as a groupstamp, after each such line the lines layout’s groupCrossSections draws across the frame holding that line’s card (none on a line in no frame), each at its piece’s opacity; then each of its anchors. Without groupstampAt no coordinate is taken for a groupstamp. Nothing for a number the index does not have.
