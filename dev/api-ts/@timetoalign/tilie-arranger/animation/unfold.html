pairRows()#

pairRows(from, to): ReadonlyMap<string, RowMotion>

Defined in: arranger/src/animation/unfold.ts:68

The motion of every row of to, the end drawing, keyed by its row’s key, against from, the drawing the motion starts from. A row whose key from holds is paired with the first such row of from and shifted by its ordinate less its own. A row from lacks enters, shifted as the paired rows around it: by the paired rows’ ordinates in to, the shift interpolated linearly between the nearest paired row above it and the nearest below, the nearest one’s beyond the first or the last, 0 where no row is paired. A key repeated in to keeps its first row.

startOf()#

startOf(from, to, offset, x): number

Defined in: arranger/src/animation/unfold.ts:205

Where the motion starts the abscissa x of the end drawing, whose card draws through to: at the abscissa the start drawing’s card draws the same position through from, plus offset (the move of the place origin between the two drawings, so both are read on the canvas); x itself moved by offset where either drawing has no knots. An abscissa beyond to’s first or last knot starts as far beyond that knot’s start, so a card’s names and padding travel with its line’s ends.
