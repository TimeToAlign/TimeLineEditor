blockMotion()#

blockMotion(before, after, measure): BlockMotion

Defined in: arranger/src/animation/elide.ts:581

What moves when a block is drawn after instead of before: first every item whose place changed travels whole (itemMotions), its contents taking no motion of their own; then each card both draw whose elisions changed (its own, or its frame’s for a member card) moves sideways (sidewaysMotion), each frame whose group elisions changed with it (frameMotion); each ancestor card only after draws, whose member card before drew, unfolds out of it (unfoldingMotion); each ancestor card only before draws, whose member card after draws, folds back into it, read in before and moved by the change of the place origin. Where the items start or stop standing side by side, an ancestor card drawn only after fades in and one drawn only before fades out instead. Nothing else moves.

cardKnots()#

cardKnots(manifest, drawing, card): Knot[]

Defined in: arranger/src/animation/elide.ts:177

The knots of the card card of drawing: both ends of every line layout records in it (lineAxes, one per run of an elided line), an end at a seam whose position lies on the other run marked across (holdsAt), and both edges of every gap of its own (not a carried one, whose coordinates lie on another tree), an edge at a position a run holds at another abscissa marked across too (a gap ending at a seam, the row crossing it whole), each at the position it draws on the card’s root, sorted by abscissa.

itemMotions()#

itemMotions(before, after): CardMotion[]

Defined in: arranger/src/animation/elide.ts:543

How every item of after (a group’s frame, an ungrouped root’s card) whose place on the canvas before drew elsewhere moves: each element of its range translated, from where before drew the item (its place less the place origin’s, so a moved origin is no move) to where after draws it; an item drawn at the same place moves nothing.

sidewaysMotion()#

sidewaysMotion(before, was, after, card, measure): CardMotion

Defined in: arranger/src/animation/elide.ts:346

How the card card of the drawing after moves from the card under the same place key before draws (was): rows kept but where elision labels shown or hidden move them (rowShifts, the card top’s move for an element of no row), each element starting where before draws the positions its extent draws (startOf over both cards’ knots, the move of the place origin added) and shifted down by the move of the card’s top (sideways).

unfoldingMotion()#

unfoldingMotion(drawn, card, measure): CardMotion

Defined in: arranger/src/animation/elide.ts:448

How the ancestor card card of drawn unfolds out of the member card it is aligned to when it is shown: each element travels down as a raise moves it (raiseUnfold: its row’s shift, entering where its row does or it has none, and where it lies in a gap: a gap’s ellipses and labels) and sideways from the member’s line in the member card, which draws the member’s span at the card’s own abscissas: what lies left or right of that span starts at the member line’s start or end and opens out of it.
